I went through your site the way a couple planning their wedding would. Here's what I saw.

The short version

Your site tells Google exactly what you are, a Las Vegas wedding chapel, and it has real online booking, which a lot of chapels don't. The weak spots are speed and a few mixed signals that can make a couple stop and wonder before they book.

What I found

01Costing you most

The homepage is slow to show its main image.

What I saw

In a simulated slow-connection load test, the first content appeared at 3.6 seconds, but the main image took 20.7 seconds. The page weighed about 3.4 MB.

Why it matters

Couples often book on short notice and compare a few chapels at once, so a long wait is an easy reason to go back and tap the next one.

The fix

Resize and compress the homepage images so the main picture shows first.

02Worth fixing

Two different phone numbers.

What I saw

The homepage lists +1 (866) 814-5830, but its “Get In Touch” call button dials (702) 405-9277.

Why it matters

Someone who saves one number and later sees the other may not be sure which is really you, or may reach a line nobody's watching.

The fix

Pick one number and use it everywhere on the site.

03Worth fixing

Booking is there, but it's a step away.

What I saw

The homepage has no booking form or calendar of its own. The “Book Online” buttons send people to a separate page, which opens with notes about minimum packages, calling for large parties and taxes not being included before the calendar starts.

Why it matters

That's all useful, but every hurdle before picking a date loses some couples who were ready to commit.

The fix

Put the calendar first and keep those notes close by, so a couple can pick a date right away.

04Quick cleanup

Smaller cleanup.

What I saw

My accessibility check found one piece of text that's hard to read against its background, and links inside paragraphs that are hard to tell apart from the surrounding text. The site also uses some outdated browser features and sets third-party cookies, which is why its overall quality score came in lower.

Why it matters

These are small, but they add up for visitors and for how the site is scored.
