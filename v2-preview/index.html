What I found

01Costing you most

The homepage is slow to show its main image.

What I saw

In a simulated slow-connection load test, the first content appeared at 3.6 seconds, but the main image took 20.7 seconds. The page weighed about 3.4 MB.

Why it matters

Couples often book on short notice and compare a few chapels at once, so a long wait is an easy reason to go back and tap the next one.

The fix

Resize and compress the homepage images so the main picture shows first.

02Worth fixing

Two different phone numbers.

What I saw

The homepage lists +1 (866) 814-5830, but its “Get In Touch” call button dials (702) 405-9277.

Why it matters

Someone who saves one number and later sees the other may not be sure which is really you, or may reach a line nobody's watching.

The fix

Pick one number and use it everywhere on the site.

03Worth fixing

Booking is there, but it's a step away.

What I saw

The homepage has no booking form or calendar of its own. The “Book Online” buttons send people to a separate page, which opens with notes about minimum packages, calling for large parties and taxes not being included before the calendar starts.

Why it matters

That's all useful, but every hurdle before picking a date loses some couples who were ready to commit.

The fix

Put the calendar first and keep those notes close by, so a couple can pick a date right away.

04Quick cleanup

Smaller cleanup.

What I saw

My accessibility check found one piece of text that's hard to read against its background, and links inside paragraphs that are hard to tell apart from the surrounding text. The site also uses some outdated browser features and sets third-party cookies, which is why its overall quality score came in lower.

Why it matters

These are small, but they add up for visitors and for how the site is scored.

What I checked on your site

  • Found
    Not blocking AI crawlers

    Your robots.txt only blocks the WordPress admin area. I tested 11 well-known AI and search crawler names (GPTBot, ChatGPT-User, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended, CCBot, Bytespider, Amazonbot) and none is blocked.

  • Found
    An llms.txt file exists

    luckylittlechapel.com/llms.txt is live. It looks auto-generated by your SEO plugin and lists your main pages and posts.

  • Found
    Page text is readable without scripts

    The homepage HTML contains about 1,500 words of text and one clear heading ("Las Vegas Wedding Chapel"), so a crawler that doesn't run JavaScript can still read it.

  • Found
    Your Google listing is linked

    The site links to your Google Maps listing and embeds the map, and your structured data lists it, along with Facebook, Instagram, YouTube and X.

  • Gap
    Structured data doesn't describe the business

    Your pages tell search tools you are an organization and a website, but not a local business or venue, and no phone number, address or hours are included in that data. Those facts only exist as plain page text.

  • Gap
    Two phone numbers

    The homepage text shows (866) 814-5830 while the call button dials (702) 405-9277. Search tools that cross-check a business's details may not know which one is right. Your address (321 South Casino Center Blvd #110) is the same on the three pages I checked.

  • Note
    Key answers sit on other pages

    Walk-ins, being across from the courthouse and the 30% deposit are covered on your FAQ page, but not in the homepage text.

These are plain checks anyone can repeat on October 15, 2026: what the site publishes, not a score.

What I couldn't measure

  • Rankings in Google or Google Maps.
  • What ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity or Google's AI answers say when asked directly. I didn't have access to ask them, so the searches above are a stand-in.
  • How many people search for these terms or how many visit your site.
  • Your Google listing itself: its reviews, photos, hours and phone number as Google shows them.

What I'd fix first

Add local-business structured data with one phone number, your address and hours, then put walk-ins, the courthouse across the street and your starting price in the first screen of the homepage text. That gives search tools clean facts to quote. I can't promise where it will place you.

What's working
